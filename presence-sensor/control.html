Control Logic

Presence and daylight are inputs; Home Assistant makes short-lived decisions and commands the purchased actuator. No raw history is needed.

Do not use live lux feedback

Never implement if presence and lux < threshold: light_on else light_off continuously. The light changes the sensor's own lux reading and can create rapid oscillation.

Recommended rules

EventLightFanReason

Manual behaviour

Keep the actuator's normal wall control or supplied remote. A manual ON should not be immediately cancelled by the automation; use an override helper or a short manual hold-off in Home Assistant. If the SBC or internet fails, the physical control must still operate the actuator.
