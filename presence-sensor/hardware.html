Hardware & Buying Guide

Buy the low-voltage sensor parts and the exact supported AZIOT actuator. Keep the sensor low-voltage and have an electrician install the faceplate switch.

Current status — AZIOT candidate, confirmation pending

AZIOT 4 Node Plus is now the leading India-market candidate. Its 4-node switch family is listed by tuya-local, and the completed AZIOT support record exposes four on/off switch datapoints. The Indian listing is AZIOT 4 Node Plus on Flipkart (observed around ₹1,642). Do not pay until the Indian developer confirms that this exact revision is compatible.

Actuator decision

The AZIOT 4 Node Plus is the only current purchase candidate. Its exact revision remains subject to developer confirmation and the local/offline and electrical checks below.

Checks before paying

  1. Confirm the exact printed model and Tuya product ID, not just the seller’s title.
  2. Confirm the repository configuration exposes the required local on/off relay entities.
  3. Confirm 220–240 V, 50 Hz, fan/motor suitability, LED inrush capability, and manual wall operation.
  4. Obtain the local key and test on a bench with the actuator in a safe enclosure.
  5. Block WAN access while keeping LAN available and run an overnight test.
  6. Only after all tests pass should an electrician install it.

Sensor construction

Use the LD2410 over UART, an LDR divider on an ADC input, and ESPHome’s native API. Report only presence, ambient_dark, and health/connectivity state. Do not stream raw radar gates or retain lux history in normal operation. Calibrate in the final room after mounting at approximately 2.2 m, angled across the room and away from the fan.

Safe sourcing

Use Indian distributors for the sensor electronics. For the actuator, wait for confirmation from the Indian tuya-local developer contacted for this project. Confirm the exact revision, local on/off entities, wiring, and motor-load suitability before buying.
