Three patterns, one server, three switch plates

A pattern-based automation reference for an Indian apartment. Identifying three uniform room patterns: timer-driven hall lighting and fan (no sensors needed), mmWave radar-driven study light and fan, and door-triggered bathroom lighting and exhaust fan. Each room pattern has its own in-wall Tinxy 4+1 Fan Smart Switch plate preserving physical wall switches, orchestrated locally by Home Assistant on a Radxa Zero 3E.

India-specific notes

  • Neutral at the switchboard. Most Indian switchboards have neutral at the plate, but older wiring may not. Check before buying the Tinxy modules, they need neutral to power their onboard ESP8266 and relays (O-11).
  • Fan load. Ceiling fans draw 60–75 W running but far more on start. Confirm the Tinxy capacitor regulator rating covers the motor inrush, not just steady current.
  • 230 V / 50 Hz. Everything here is rated for Indian domestic mains. Tinxy is engineered in Bengaluru for Indian modular boxes.
  • Mains work needs an electrician. Low-voltage sensor work is yours. The wall plate is not.
