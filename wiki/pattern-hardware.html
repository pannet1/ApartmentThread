Hardware by Automation Pattern

Mapping physical switch plates, sensors, and microcontrollers directly to the three Apartment Thread automation patterns: Hall (timer-driven, zero sensors), Study Room (presence-driven light & fan), and Bathroom (door-triggered light & exhaust fan), alongside the Central Hub border router stack.

Uniform Switch Architecture Across Patterns

To keep the automation architecture uniform across all rooms, every pattern incorporates a Tinxy 4 Node Smart Switch with Fan Regulators (4 lights/appliances + 1 fan speed regulator). Sourced directly from tinxy.in alongside verified Indian suppliers (Robu.in and Robocraze).

1. Hall Pattern Hardware (Timer-Driven)

The Hallway pattern (HL-01) relies on deterministic solar angle and schedule triggers (sunset lighting, bedtime dimming) without physical presence sensors:

  • In-Wall Switch Plate: 1× Tinxy 4+1 Fan Smart Switch (₹1,299) fitted inside the hallway modular switch box. Delivers local relay control over the hallway lighting circuits and electronic capacitor fan speed regulation.
  • Sensors: Zero sensors required (₹0). Bypasses false presence triggers caused by passing foot traffic, pets, or wide hallway draft angles.
  • Pattern Cost: ₹1,299 total.

2. Study Room Pattern Hardware (Presence-Driven)

The Study Room pattern (ST-01) automates desk lighting and ceiling fan based on micromotion detection (typing, breathing, sedentary work):

  • In-Wall Switch Plate: 1× Tinxy 4+1 Fan Smart Switch (₹1,299) behind the study modular plate.
  • Presence Sensor (PS-1 Kit):
    • HLK-LD2410S 24GHz mmWave radar (₹639) for fine stationary target tracking.
    • Seeed Studio XIAO BLE nRF52840 (₹1,149) running Sentinel Matter-over-Thread firmware.
    • Witty Fox 3.7V 2,000 mAh LiPo cell (₹256) delivering 18–24 months battery life via TPS62840 power gating.
    • Custom Sentinel PCB, ST LDLN030G33R LDO, passives, and 3D tilt bracket (~₹418). Specs in PS-1 Component Guide.
  • Pattern Cost: ₹3,761 total (₹1,299 switch + ₹2,462 sensor kit).

3. Bathroom Pattern Hardware (Door-Triggered)

The Bathroom pattern (BA-01) uses a door contact state machine to control vanity lighting and exhaust ventilation, eliminating false dropouts inside shower enclosures:

  • In-Wall Switch Plate: 1× Tinxy 4+1 Fan Smart Switch (₹1,299) controlling light and exhaust fan.
  • Door Contact Sensor (DS-1 Kit):
    • Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C6 (₹672) running Matter-over-Thread firmware on the same mesh.
    • MC-38 Magnetic Reed Switch (₹57) mounted on door leaf and exterior jamb.
    • 2,000 mAh LiPo battery (₹256) delivering 4–5 year service life. Specs in DS-1 Component Guide.
  • Pattern Cost: ₹2,284 total (₹1,299 switch + ₹985 DIY Matter kit).

4. Central Hub Hardware (Server & Thread Mesh)

The centralized backbone hosting Home Assistant and bridging the 802.15.4 mesh network:

  • Border Router Radio (BR-1): M5Stack Module Gateway H2 (₹889) pre-flashed with OpenThread RCP firmware, connected via 3.3V UART (BR-1 Specs).
  • Local Server (SRV-1): Radxa Zero 3E (RK3566, Gigabit Ethernet) running Debian 13 Minimal & Docker on on-hand hardware (SRV-1 Specs).
  • Hub Cost: ₹889 new spend (server and power supply on hand).
