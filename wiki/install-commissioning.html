Stage 5 — Mounting, Tuning & Automation

The final integration. Mounting sensors at their surveyed physical positions, calibrating radar energy thresholds against spinning ceiling fans, activating room automations, and verifying power-cut resilience.

Stage 5 Objective

Deploy physical hardware to wall mounts, fine-tune the LD2410 mmWave radar distance gates to eliminate false triggers from ceiling fans, activate the room state machines (HL-01, ST-01, BA-01), and verify that all systems cold-boot cleanly after a complete power failure.

1. Physical Sensor Mounting & Geometry

Mount the benched sensors at their surveyed apartment positions according to the Radar Placement Guide:

  • Study Room (PS-1 Presence Sensor): Mount on the interior wall at a height of 1.8 meters, tilted downward by 15 degrees directly facing the study desk chair. Ensure the radar beam avoids door swing arcs to prevent hallway walk-by triggers.
  • Bathroom (DS-1 Door Sensor): Mount the XIAO ESP32-C6 transmitter on the door exterior frame, and the magnetic reed target on the moving door leaf. Keep the gap under 15 mm when the door is latched.

2. Ceiling Fan Radar Calibration

Ceiling fan blade rotation is the #1 cause of phantom presence triggers in Indian apartments. Tune out fan vibrations using the LD2410 gate thresholds:

  1. Power ON the Study Room ceiling fan at maximum speed (Speed 5). Ensure no humans or moving objects are in the room.
  2. Open the Home Assistant LD2410 diagnostic entities (or open the HLKRadarTool app via Bluetooth).
  3. Inspect the real-time energy readouts across distance gates (each gate represents 0.75 meters):
    • Identify which gates exhibit energy spikes from the spinning fan blades (typically Gate 2: 1.5m, or Gate 3: 2.25m).
    • Note the peak stationary energy value reported by the fan (e.g. energy level 38).
  4. Adjust the Stationary Threshold for that specific gate to 15 points above the fan's peak reading (e.g. set threshold to 55).
  5. Sit in the study desk chair. Confirm that normal sedentary breathing produces energy well above 55 (typically 75–90), maintaining steady presence without dropping out.
  6. Set the Occupancy Delay / Hold Time to 90 seconds to bridge brief postures or head turns.

4. Power Outage Cold-Boot Resilience Test

In Indian power grids, power cuts and inverter transfers occur frequently. Test cold-boot recovery:

  1. Trip the main apartment lighting breaker for 30 seconds, simulating a complete power failure.
  2. Restore mains power.
  3. Observe system recovery:
    • Radxa Zero 3E powers up; Armbian boots into systemd in ~25 seconds.
    • Docker Engine starts; homeassistant and otbr containers auto-start under --restart=unless-stopped.
    • The ESP32-H2 radio initializes and OTBR restores the ApartmentThread mesh within 45 seconds.
    • Tinxy Wi-Fi switchboards reconnect to the local router and resume local communication.
    • Battery-powered PS-1 and DS-1 sensors reattach to their parent router automatically.
  4. Confirm all three room automations function correctly within 60 seconds of power restoration without manual intervention.
