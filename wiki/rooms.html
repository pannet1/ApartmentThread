Room Automation Patterns

Three rooms, three distinct automation patterns. We identify reusable architectural patterns for Indian apartments: timer-driven hall lighting without sensors, mmWave presence-driven study light and fan, and door contact-triggered bathroom lighting and ventilation.

Entities & Pattern Mapping

Each room pattern is equipped with its own dedicated in-wall Tinxy 4+1 Fan Smart Switch controlling local lights and fan, paired with that pattern's specific trigger mechanism:

1. The Hall Pattern — Timer-Driven Lighting (No Sensor)

Pattern Philosophy — Zero Sensor Cost

Hallways and living rooms have high transient foot traffic, wide open sightlines, and roaming pets where radar sensors often create nuisance triggers and drain batteries. No presence sensor is required. Instead, deterministic time/schedule automations trigger the Tinxy switch to turn the light on and off, with the physical wall switch always ready for manual use.

Home Assistant YAML

alias: "Hall Light: Evening ON"
trigger:
  - platform: sun
    event: sunset
    offset: "-00:15:00"
  - platform: time
    at: "18:30:00"
action:
  - service: switch.turn_on
    target:
      entity_id: switch.hall_light
mode: single

alias: "Hall Light: Night OFF"
trigger:
  - platform: time
    at: "23:00:00"
action:
  - service: switch.turn_off
    target:
      entity_id: switch.hall_light
mode: single

Timers handle routine evening illumination predictably without sensor latency or false positives. Outside scheduled hours, the occupant simply taps the physical wall switch on the Tinxy plate.

2. The Study Room Pattern — Presence-Driven Light & Fan

Pattern Philosophy — Stationary Occupant Detection

In a study room or home office, people sit stationary for long hours reading, typing, or on video calls. Traditional PIR sensors fail here because they only detect bulk motion, plunging the occupant into darkness. The Sentinel mmWave presence sensor (PS-1) detects micro-movements (breathing, typing). Presence turns both the light and the fan ON; vacating the room turns both OFF.

Home Assistant YAML

alias: "Study Room: Occupied -> Light & Fan ON"
trigger:
  - platform: state
    entity_id: binary_sensor.study_presence
    to: "on"
action:
  - service: switch.turn_on
    target:
      entity_id:
        - switch.study_light
        - switch.study_fan
mode: single

alias: "Study Room: Vacated -> Light & Fan OFF"
trigger:
  - platform: state
    entity_id: binary_sensor.study_presence
    to: "off"
    for: "00:02:00"
action:
  - service: switch.turn_off
    target:
      entity_id:
        - switch.study_light
        - switch.study_fan
mode: single

The 2-minute for: "00:02:00" vacancy buffer ensures that brief micro-shadows or momentary step-outs do not prematurely turn off the light and fan.

3. The Bathroom Pattern — Door Contact-Driven Light & Exhaust Fan

Pattern Philosophy — Physical Boundary Determinism

Bathrooms possess an absolute physical boundary: the door. When an occupant enters and closes the door, occupancy is certain. When the door is opened, the occupant is either entering (light turns on immediately so they never step into darkness) or exiting (grace timer begins). The low-latency door contact sensor (DS-1) triggers the bathroom's Tinxy 4+1 switch directly to turn the light and exhaust fan on and off.

The Automation State Machine — Solving Entry & Exit

A naive rule like "Door closed → Light ON; Door open → Light OFF" fails when someone enters: they open the door, step in, and are left in pitch darkness until they pull the door shut! The Home Assistant state machine resolves all 4 states cleanly:

Bathroom Presence State Machine
  1. State 1 — Entry Trigger (Door Transitions CLOSED → OPEN): Light (switch.bathroom_light) turns ON immediately (<50ms). The room illuminates before the occupant steps over the threshold. A 60-second entry timer starts.
  2. State 2 — Occupied Latch (Door Transitions OPEN → CLOSED): The occupant is inside. The entry timer is cancelled. Light and exhaust fan (fan.bathroom_exhaust_fan) are locked ON continuously. No stationary timeouts will ever leave a showering occupant in the dark.
  3. State 3 — Exit Trigger (Door Transitions CLOSED → OPEN): The occupant finishes and opens the door to exit. A 60-second vacancy countdown begins.
  4. State 4 — Vacated (Timer Expires OR Door Re-closed during Exit): Light and fan turn OFF automatically.
  5. 45-Minute Safety Watchdog: If the door is accidentally shut while vacant, HA turns the load off after 45 minutes to prevent wasted electricity.

Moisture & Sensor Placement Guidelines

  1. Exterior Door Frame Placement: Mount the active sensor body (DS-1 containing MCU/battery) on the outer or top edge of the door frame, not inside the wet shower splash zone. Only the passive magnet piece mounts to the moving door leaf.
  2. Gap Tolerance: Ensure the alignment gap when the door is shut is ≤15 mm (the Sonoff SNZB-04P supports up to 20 mm).
  3. Steam & Condensation: In Indian bathrooms with geysers, steam rises. Mounting the sensor at mid-height on the exterior door jamb avoids condensation pools.

Home Assistant YAML

# 1. Door Opens -> Light ON Immediately (Illuminate before entering)
alias: "Bathroom: Door Opened -> Light ON Immediately"
trigger:
  - platform: state
    entity_id: binary_sensor.bathroom_door_contact
    to: "on"                       # "on" represents physically open
action:
  - service: switch.turn_on
    target:
      entity_id: switch.bathroom_light
mode: single

# 2. Door Closes -> Occupancy Latched (Light & Exhaust Fan ON continuously)
alias: "Bathroom: Door Closed -> Occupancy Latched ON"
trigger:
  - platform: state
    entity_id: binary_sensor.bathroom_door_contact
    to: "off"                      # "off" represents physically closed/latched
action:
  - service: switch.turn_on
    target:
      entity_id: switch.bathroom_light
  - service: fan.turn_on
    target:
      entity_id: fan.bathroom_exhaust_fan
mode: single

# 3. Door Left Open / Exit -> Vacancy Grace Countdown -> Turn OFF
alias: "Bathroom: Door Opened / Vacated -> Turn OFF After Grace Period"
trigger:
  - platform: state
    entity_id: binary_sensor.bathroom_door_contact
    to: "on"
    for: "00:01:00"                # 60-second grace period for exit / ventilation
action:
  - service: switch.turn_off
    target:
      entity_id: switch.bathroom_light
  - service: fan.turn_off
    target:
      entity_id: fan.bathroom_exhaust_fan
mode: single

Manual Control — All Patterns

The Wall Switch Always Wins

If Home Assistant, Wi-Fi, Thread, or the Radxa SBC is offline, every light and fan must still operate instantaneously from the physical wall switches. The Tinxy 4+1 Smart Switch plate preserves full physical switch mechanical/relay control independently of microcontroller logic (O-18).
