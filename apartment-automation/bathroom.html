Bathroom Input & Switch

The bathroom automation pattern. Uniformly controlled by an in-wall Aziot 3+1 Fan Smart Switch plate driving both the bathroom lighting and exhaust fan, triggered deterministically by a low-latency door contact sensor on the bathroom door.

Component Identity — Decoupled Architecture

Aziot 3+1 Fan Smart Switch (Actuator) + Matter-over-Thread Contact Sensor (Input):
• The Switch is Fixed: The in-wall Aziot 3+1 switch plate controls the physical 230V loads (lights & exhaust fan) and maintains manual wall switches 24/7.
• The Sensor Sends the Trigger to Home Assistant: The bathroom door contact sensor operates completely wirelessly over Matter over Thread, transmitting state changes (binary_sensor.bathroom_door_contact) to Home Assistant via the M5Stack ESP32-H2 Thread Border Router.
• Home Assistant Automates the Aziot: Home Assistant receives the Matter trigger in <50ms, runs the occupancy state machine, and commands the Aziot switch (switch.bathroom_light, fan.bathroom_exhaust_fan) locally.

Engineering Analysis: The "Thinking" Behind Door Contact Sensors

A door sensor seems trivial until deployed for room lighting. In a bathroom, where occupants enter in the dark, shower with high humidity, and operate switches with wet hands, poor sensor selection leads to instant usability failure or electrical hazards.

1. The Latency Trap — Why Battery Wi-Fi Sensors Fail for Lighting

Never use battery-powered Wi-Fi door sensors (Tuya Wi-Fi, Sonoff DW2 Wi-Fi) for lighting automation:
• The Cold Boot RF Sequence: To preserve battery, a Wi-Fi sensor turns off its 2.4 GHz radio in deep sleep. On door opening, the microcontroller wakes up → powers RF synthesizer → scans 2.4 GHz channels (800–1,500 ms) → associates with Wi-Fi AP → performs WPA2 4-way handshake (600–1,000 ms) → requests DHCP IP lease (500–1,200 ms) → opens TCP socket and publishes MQTT state (300–500 ms).
• Total Latency: 2.5 to 5.0 seconds! In practice, an occupant opens the door at night, walks into total pitch darkness, fumbles for the door, and waits 3 seconds before the light illuminates. Humans instinctive switch reaction time is <300 ms. By 1,000 ms, occupants conclude automation is broken and jab the physical wall switch.
• Rapid Battery Death: High-power Wi-Fi wakes draw 80–150 mA for 3 seconds. In a family bathroom with 25–35 actuations daily, coin-cell or AAA batteries are exhausted in 6 to 8 weeks.

2. The Mains Isolation Barrier — Aziot Switchboard vs Door Frame

Never connect a wired reed switch directly to the Aziot 3+1 switch plate terminals:
• The Aziot 3+1 Fan Smart Switch is a 230V AC mains-powered modular unit. Its physical switch inputs operate at line voltage (Phase 230V).
• Running thin 2-core wire from an unisolated 230V AC switchboard terminal to a magnetic reed switch mounted on a metal door frame or tiled bathroom wall creates a lethal shock hazard (especially with wet hands/feet) and directly violates Indian Electrical Standard IS 732.
• Strict Rule: Any wired magnetic dry contact (e.g., MC-38) must terminate only at an isolated, low-voltage 3.3V DC GPIO (such as the Radxa Zero 3E 40-pin GPIO header or an isolated low-voltage ESPHome node), never inside the mains switchboard.

Unifying with Matter over Thread: The "Twin Sensor" Approach

Architectural Harmony — 100% Matter over Thread

Yes, the bathroom contact sensor can work identically to the Study Room presence sensor using Matter over Thread!
In fact, adopting Matter over Thread for the bathroom door sensor creates the cleanest possible architecture:
• Single Border Router: The M5Stack ESP32-H2 serves as the solitary OpenThread Border Router (OTBR) for your entire apartment.
• Zero Protocol Fragmentation: Eliminates the need for a separate Zigbee coordinator (ZHA / Zigbee2MQTT). Every sensor in the house communicates via native IPv6 Thread packets directly into Home Assistant's Matter integration.
• Unified Commissioning: Both the presence sensor and the door sensor are paired using standard Matter QR codes.

Option 1: How to Build the DIY Matter-over-Thread Contact Sensor (Hardware Twin)

You can build a battery-powered Matter-over-Thread door sensor using the exact same miniature form-factor as the Sentinel presence sensor:
• Microcontroller: Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C6 → (₹672 on Robocraze) or Seeed Studio XIAO BLE nRF52840 → (₹1,149). Both feature onboard 802.15.4 Thread radios and integrated LiPo battery chargers.
• Contact Element: An MC-38 Magnetic Reed Switch → (₹57) connected between a digital GPIO pin (Pin D1 / GPIO2) and GND with an internal pull-up resistor.
• Power Source: A single-cell Witty Fox 3.7V 2,000 mAh Prismatic Battery → (₹256 on Robocraze) with built-in protection PCB, soldered to the underside battery pads (BAT+ and BAT-).
• Sleepy End Device (SED) Power Profile: Unlike the presence sensor which powers a 24 GHz radar, a door contact has zero active sensing load. The MCU remains in deep sleep drawing <5 μA. On door movement, the reed switch generates a hardware GPIO interrupt, wakes the chip in <15 ms, transmits the Matter BooleanState report over Thread in <35 ms, and returns to deep sleep. A 2,000 mAh battery will last 4 to 5+ years.
• Total DIY Build Cost: ₹672 (XIAO C6) + ₹57 (MC-38) + ₹256 (Battery) = ₹985 (consolidated in the same Robocraze cart as the XIAO boards!).

The Automation State Machine — Solving Entry & Exit

A naive rule like "Door closed → Light ON; Door open → Light OFF" fails when someone enters: they open the door, step in, and are left in pitch darkness until they pull the door shut! Furthermore, if they close the door behind them upon exiting, the light turns back on. The robust Home Assistant state machine resolves all 4 states:

Bathroom Presence State Machine
  1. State 1 — Entry Trigger (Door Transitions CLOSED → OPEN): Light (switch.bathroom_light) turns ON immediately. The room illuminates before the occupant steps over the threshold. A 60-second entry timer starts.
  2. State 2 — Occupied Latch (Door Transitions OPEN → CLOSED): The occupant is inside. The entry timer is cancelled. Light and exhaust fan (fan.bathroom_exhaust_fan) are locked ON continuously. No stationary timeouts will ever leave a showering or seated occupant in the dark.
  3. State 3 — Exit Trigger (Door Transitions CLOSED → OPEN): The occupant finishes and opens the door to exit. A 60-second vacancy countdown begins.
  4. State 4 — Vacated (Timer Expires OR Door Re-closed during Exit): Light and fan turn OFF automatically.
  5. Failsafe Watchdog & Manual Override:
    • The wall switch always wins: Tapping the physical switch on the Aziot 3+1 plate overrides automation immediately (O-18).
    • 45-Minute Safety Watchdog: If the door is accidentally shut while vacant, HA turns the load off after 45 minutes to prevent wasted electricity.

Mounting & Moisture Guidelines

  1. Exterior Door Frame Placement: Mount the active sensor body (containing the electronics/battery) on the outer or top edge of the door frame, not inside the wet shower splash zone. Only the passive magnet piece mounts to the moving door leaf.
  2. Gap Tolerance: Ensure the alignment gap when the door is shut is ≤15 mm (the SNZB-04P supports up to 20 mm).
  3. Steam & Condensation: In Indian bathrooms with geysers, steam rises. Mounting the sensor at mid-height on the exterior door jamb avoids condensation pools.

Validation Gates

  1. O-12 decides the sensor path. Wireless Zigbee (SNZB-04P) for clean installation without drilling, vs Wired MC-38 if Radxa GPIO is physically accessible. Evidence that closes it.
  2. Verify the binary sensor entity flips cleanly in Home Assistant: off = contact closed (door shut), on = contact open (door ajar).
  3. Confirm physical wall switch override operates offline (O-18) without automation fighting the user.
