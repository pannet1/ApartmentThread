Apartment Automation Patterns

Three rooms, three distinct automation patterns. We are identifying reusable architectural patterns for Indian apartments: timer-driven hall lighting without sensors, mmWave presence-driven study light and fan, and door-triggered bathroom lighting.

Entities & Pattern Mapping

Each room pattern is equipped with its own dedicated in-wall Aziot 3+1 Fan Smart Switch controlling local lights and fan, paired with that pattern's specific trigger mechanism:

1. The Hall Pattern — Timer-Driven Lighting (No Sensor)

Pattern Philosophy — Zero Sensor Cost

Hallways and living rooms have high transient foot traffic, wide open sightlines, and roaming pets where radar sensors often create nuisance triggers and drain batteries. No presence sensor is required. Instead, deterministic time/schedule automations trigger the Aziot switch to turn the light on and off, with the physical wall switch always ready for manual use.

Home Assistant YAML

alias: "Hall Light: Evening ON"
trigger:
  - platform: sun
    event: sunset
    offset: "-00:15:00"
  - platform: time
    at: "18:30:00"
action:
  - service: switch.turn_on
    target:
      entity_id: switch.hall_light
mode: single

alias: "Hall Light: Night OFF"
trigger:
  - platform: time
    at: "23:00:00"
action:
  - service: switch.turn_off
    target:
      entity_id: switch.hall_light
mode: single

Timers handle routine evening illumination predictably without sensor latency or false positives. Outside scheduled hours, the occupant simply taps the physical wall switch on the Aziot plate.

2. The Study Room Pattern — Presence-Driven Light & Fan

Pattern Philosophy — Stationary Occupant Detection

In a study room or home office, people sit stationary for long hours reading, typing, or on video calls. Traditional PIR sensors fail here because they only detect bulk motion, plunging the occupant into darkness. The Sentinel mmWave presence sensor (HLK-LD2410S) detects micro-movements (breathing, typing). Presence turns both the light and the fan ON; vacating the room turns both OFF.

Home Assistant YAML

alias: "Study Room: Occupied -> Light & Fan ON"
trigger:
  - platform: state
    entity_id: binary_sensor.study_presence
    to: "on"
action:
  - service: switch.turn_on
    target:
      entity_id:
        - switch.study_light
        - switch.study_fan
mode: single

alias: "Study Room: Vacated -> Light & Fan OFF"
trigger:
  - platform: state
    entity_id: binary_sensor.study_presence
    to: "off"
    for: "00:02:00"
action:
  - service: switch.turn_off
    target:
      entity_id:
        - switch.study_light
        - switch.study_fan
mode: single

The 2-minute for: "00:02:00" vacancy buffer ensures that brief micro-shadows or momentary step-outs do not prematurely turn off the light and fan.

3. The Bathroom Pattern — Door Contact-Driven Light & Exhaust Fan

Pattern Philosophy — Physical Boundary Determinism

Bathrooms possess a distinct physical boundary: the door. When an occupant enters and closes the door, occupancy is certain. When the door is opened, the occupant is either entering (light turns on immediately so they never step into darkness) or exiting (grace timer begins). The low-latency door contact sensor triggers the bathroom's Aziot 3+1 switch directly to turn the light and exhaust fan on and off.

Home Assistant YAML

# 1. Door Opens -> Light ON Immediately (Illuminate before entering)
alias: "Bathroom: Door Opened -> Light ON Immediately"
trigger:
  - platform: state
    entity_id: binary_sensor.bathroom_door_contact
    to: "on"                       # "on" represents physically open
action:
  - service: switch.turn_on
    target:
      entity_id: switch.bathroom_light
mode: single

# 2. Door Closes -> Occupancy Latched (Light & Exhaust Fan ON continuously)
alias: "Bathroom: Door Closed -> Occupancy Latched ON"
trigger:
  - platform: state
    entity_id: binary_sensor.bathroom_door_contact
    to: "off"                      # "off" represents physically closed/latched
action:
  - service: switch.turn_on
    target:
      entity_id: switch.bathroom_light
  - service: fan.turn_on
    target:
      entity_id: fan.bathroom_exhaust_fan
mode: single

# 3. Door Left Open / Exit -> Vacancy Grace Countdown -> Turn OFF
alias: "Bathroom: Door Opened / Vacated -> Turn OFF After Grace Period"
trigger:
  - platform: state
    entity_id: binary_sensor.bathroom_door_contact
    to: "on"
    for: "00:01:00"                # 60-second grace period for exit / ventilation
action:
  - service: switch.turn_off
    target:
      entity_id: switch.bathroom_light
  - service: fan.turn_off
    target:
      entity_id: fan.bathroom_exhaust_fan
mode: single

Opening the door turns the light ON in <80ms before the occupant takes a step inside. Closing the door locks both lighting and exhaust ventilation ON indefinitely (occupant showering or seated). When the door is reopened to exit, a 60-second grace period allows exiting or air ventilation before powering off.

Manual Control — All Patterns

The Wall Switch Always Wins

If Home Assistant, Wi-Fi, Thread, or the Radxa SBC is offline, every light and fan must still operate instantaneously from the physical wall switches. The Aziot 3+1 Fan Smart Switch plate preserves full physical switch mechanical/relay control independently of microcontroller logic (O-18).
