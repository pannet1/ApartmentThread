Pattern Bill of Materials & Hardware Reference

Hardware bill of materials mapped directly to the three apartment automation patterns: Hall (timer-driven, zero sensors), Study Room (presence-driven light & fan), and Bathroom (door-triggered light & exhaust fan). Rather than assuming a single central switchboard plate, each room pattern is equipped with its own dedicated in-wall switch plate using the uniform Aziot 3+1 Fan Smart Switch controlling both lights and fan.

Uniform Switch Architecture Across Patterns

To keep the automation architecture uniform across all rooms, every pattern incorporates an Aziot 3+1 Fan Smart Switch (3 lights/switches + 1 fan speed regulator). Sourced with verified Indian supplier links (primarily Robu.in, Robocraze, and Aziot.life).
