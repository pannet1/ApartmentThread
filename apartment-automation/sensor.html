Presence Sensor

The battery device that tells Home Assistant a room is occupied. Built on the open-source Sentinel reference design (Localized Tech): HLK-LD2410S 24 GHz radar, Seeed Studio XIAO nRF52840, and native Matter-over-Thread with 4–5 months proven battery life.

Component identity — PS-1

Every presence sensor in this build is identified as PS-1: HLK-LD2410S radar + Seeed Studio XIAO nRF52840 + 2,000 mAh LiPo cell + Sentinel firmware (Matter over Thread) in an articulating ball-and-socket enclosure. Commissions natively via standard Matter QR code directly into Home Assistant — zero cloud dependency, no proprietary hubs.

Assembly & Commissioning

  1. Flash the Firmware: Connect the Seeed Studio XIAO nRF52840 to your PC via USB-C. Double-click the tiny reset button on the board — it mounts as a mass-storage drive named XIAO-SENSE. Drag and drop the precompiled zephyr.uf2 file from sentinel-firmware. The drive unmounts automatically once flashed.
  2. Wire the Hardware:
    • Radar TX → XIAO D7 (RX, UART0)
    • Radar RX → XIAO D6 (TX, UART0)
    • Radar OUT → XIAO D3 (Sensor Interrupt / SENSOR_IRQ)
    • LDO EN → XIAO D2 (Power Gate)
    • Radar VCC & GND → Regulated 3.3 V rail and common Ground
    • LiPo Battery → XIAO underside battery pads (BAT+ / BAT-)
  3. Matter Commissioning: Open Home Assistant on your phone (connected to the same local Wi-Fi / Thread network). Navigate to Settings → Devices & Services → Add Integration → Add Matter Device. Scan the Sentinel QR code (demonstrated at 3:17 in the video). Home Assistant provisions the device onto Thread immediately.
  4. Auto-Threshold Calibration: In Home Assistant, trigger the Auto Threshold entity while the room is empty (demonstrated at 4:49 in the video). The radar scans room reflections for 2 minutes to establish a noise baseline, eliminating false triggers from still furniture or curtain reflections.
  5. Enclosure & Mounting: Assemble the electronics into the 3D-printed ball-and-socket enclosure (demonstrated at 0:23 in the video). Mount on a wall at ~2.2 m height, tilted slightly downward towards the detection zone (avoid pointing directly at ceiling fans, see Radar placement).
Native Home Assistant Entities

Once paired via Matter, Sentinel natively exposes: Occupancy (binary sensor), Target Distance (sensor in meters), Auto Threshold (trigger switch), and Battery Level (percentage sensor). Discovered automatically through Home Assistant's native Matter integration.
