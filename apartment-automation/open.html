Open Items

Everything in this wiki that is claimed but not yet proven. Each one names the exact evidence that closes it. Work down the list; buy nothing that sits behind an open item marked BLOCKS PURCHASE.

How an item closes

Run the check, keep the evidence — a serial log, a screenshot, a current trace, a photo of the nameplate — then flip the status here. An intention does not close an item; a result does.
